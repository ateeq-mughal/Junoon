JUNOON STUDENT PITCH
====================

1. Unzip the folder anywhere. Keep index.html and the "assets" folder together.
2. Open index.html in Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. No internet is needed.
3. Press F for full screen.

Moving through the slides
  Next ........ Right arrow, Space, Page Down, or click the right side
  Back ........ Left arrow, Page Up, or click the left side
  First/last .. Home / End
  Clicker ..... Any presentation clicker that sends Page Up / Page Down works

Speaker notes
  N ........... Show or hide what to say, at the bottom of the screen
  P ........... Open a presenter window with notes, the next slide and a timer.
                Put the slides on the projector (full screen) and keep the
                presenter window on your laptop. Its buttons and arrow keys
                move the slides too.

Things to know
  Slide 7 plays a 10 second Ustaad video WITH SOUND. Connect the speakers.
  If it starts muted, click "Play with sound".
  Slide 8 types out an Ustaad answer by itself (about 20 seconds). Talk over it.
  Slide 15 has QR codes for Android, iPhone and the website. Leave it up for a
  minute so everyone can scan.

The whole talk takes about 12 to 15 minutes.
